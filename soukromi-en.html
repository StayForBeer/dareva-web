Photos and notes for completed challenges are visible only to you. They are stored in private storage that other users cannot access.

What your friends see: your username, level, current streak, weekly points and the joint challenges you share. They do not see your questionnaire answers, health data, notes or photos.

Steps and distance (Health Connect): we only read; we never write anything to Health Connect. Manually entered records don't count. We use data from Health Connect only to verify challenges in the app. We don't sell it, don't use it for advertising or creditworthiness decisions and don't share it with anyone apart from the server provider listed below. Our use of Health Connect data follows the Google Play policy for Health Connect, including the Limited Use requirements.

Location: we read it only while you record a route that you start yourself. A notification is visible for the whole recording, and location is never used in the background without it. Route points are sent to our server only to be checked (distance, and whether the route was driven) and are deleted straight after the check. Only the final distance in metres is saved. The route map stays only on your phone, without the first and last 150 metres, and is never shared.

Notifications (challenge reminders) are scheduled directly by your phone. Nothing is sent to our server for them.

The app currently does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics tools (apart from the crash reporting described below) and we do not sell your data to anyone. If this changes, we will update this policy in good time and let you know in the app.

3. Who processes data on our behalf

We store data with the provider Supabase, Inc. (database, login and photo storage). The data is stored on servers in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden), operated for Supabase by Amazon Web Services. Supabase is a US company, so the data may be accessed from outside the EU, for example during technical support. Such transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

The app is distributed by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited). Google processes data during installation and use according to its own policies.

Crash reporting: when the app crashes, a technical report (error description, app version, phone model and system and an anonymous account ID) is sent automatically to the Sentry service (Functional Software, Inc.). The data is stored in the European Union (Germany). The report does not contain your e-mail, name, photos or notes and is used only to fix bugs. It is deleted after 90 days at the latest.

Map tiles: route maps are displayed using the OpenFreeMap service with OpenStreetMap data. When a map is shown, your phone downloads map tiles, which reveal the displayed area and your phone's IP address. No account data or route points are sent there.

4. How long we keep data

  • We keep all data for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete all data including photos immediately. It disappears from the provider's backups within 30 days at the latest.
  • We delete health data as soon as you choose to in the app or withdraw your consent.
  • Crash reports are deleted after 90 days at the latest.
  • Route points are deleted straight after the check. Route maps are only on your phone and are deleted when you log out or under Profile → Account & privacy → Delete route maps from this phone.
  • Reports (those you sent and those about you) may be kept after your account is deleted, no longer linked to the account, only until the case is resolved.

5. Your rights

  • Access and portability: you can download all your data in the app under Profile → Account & privacy → Download my data.
  • Erasure: you can delete your account and all data under Profile → Account & privacy → Delete account. You can also do it without the app, see How to delete your account.
  • Withdrawing consent for steps and location: turn off step access in the Health Connect app and location access in your phone settings (Apps → Dareva → Permissions). You can still tick challenges off, just without the Verified label. You'll also find an explanation under Profile → Account & privacy → Activity & privacy.
  • Withdrawing consent for health data: under Profile → Account & privacy → Delete health data. Withdrawal does not affect processing before it.
  • Rectification, restriction of processing and objection: write to us at the e-mail above.
  • Complaint: you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, uoou.gov.cz, or with the data protection authority in your country.

6. Age

The app is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not allow younger people to sign up. If we find out that an account belongs to someone younger, we will delete it.

7. Security

The connection to our server is encrypted. Each user can access only their own data, which is enforced directly by the database. We do not store passwords in readable form.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new feature. We will let you know about significant changes in the app in advance. The current version is always on this page.

This policy is also available in Czech. In case of any discrepancy, the Czech version prevails.

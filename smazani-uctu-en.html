How to delete your account and data

Dareva app · operated by Klaus Pawel · Česky

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Dareva app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Account & privacy.
  3. At the bottom, in the Delete account section, type the word DELETE in the field and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. If you want to keep your data, download it first with the Download my data button on the same screen.

Without the app

Write from the e-mail you use to log in to the app to hlaseniproblemu@gmail.com with the subject Delete Dareva account. We will delete the account within 30 days and send you a confirmation.

What gets deleted with your account

Account, e-mail, username, date of birth, questionnaire answers including health data, all challenges, notes and photos, points, streaks, badges, friends, requests and joint challenges. It disappears from the provider's backups within 30 days at the latest.

What may remain for a short time:

  • Reports you sent or that concern you. We keep them without any link to your account, only until they are resolved.
  • Technical crash reports (without e-mail, name, photos and notes). They are deleted automatically after 90 days at the latest.

Deleting only part of your data (without deleting your account)

You can delete some data and keep your account:

  • Health data: in the app under Profile → Account & privacy → Delete health data. It is deleted immediately and this also withdraws your consent to its processing. Your account, challenges, points and streaks remain.
  • Anything else (for example a specific photo or note): write to hlaseniproblemu@gmail.com from the e-mail you use to log in and say what you want deleted. We will delete it within 30 days and send you a confirmation.
